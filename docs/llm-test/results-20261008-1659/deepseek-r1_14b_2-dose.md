\text{Total daily dose} = 5 \, \text{mg/kg} \times 27 \, \text{kg} = 135 \, \text{mg}

\[
\text{Dose per intake} = \frac{135 \, \text{mg}}{3} = 45 \, \text{mg}
\]

\[
\text{Difference} = 62.5 \, \text{mg} - 45 \, \text{mg} = 17.5 \, \text{mg}
\]

\[
\text{Percentage difference} = \left( \frac{17.5 \, \text{mg}}{45 \, \text{mg}} \right) \times 100\% \approx 38.89\%
\]

Thus, the closest practical dose per intake is \boxed{62.5} mg, and it is approximately \boxed{38.89\%} higher than the exact dose.
